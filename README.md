# Awesome OSINT

A [TraceLabs](https://tracelabs.org) endorsed list of awesome OSINT frameworks, libraries and software.

Note that there are great OSINT tools out there, but this list consists of tools that apply specifically to missing-persons searches.

Join us on Discord!<br>[<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Discord-%40TraceLabs-teal">](https://tracelabs.org/discord)

## 📧 Email Search

*Tools for performing email searches via social media, breach info, and other sources.*

- [Buster](https://github.com/sham00n/buster) – Advanced tool for email reconnaissance.
- [Epieos](https://epieos.com/) – Retrieve information linked to an email address (Google ID, social media, breaches).
- [Have I Been Pwned](https://haveibeenpwned.com/) – Check if an email address has been exposed in a data breach.
- [Holehe](https://github.com/megadose/holehe) – Check if an email is registered on various sites (Twitter, Instagram, Imgur, etc.).
- [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io/) – Find and verify professional email addresses associated with a domain.
- [Infoga](https://github.com/m4ll0k/Infoga) – Email information gathering tool that finds email sources from search engines and PGP servers.
- [LeakCheck](https://leakcheck.io/) – Search leaked databases by email, username, or phone number.
- [theHarvester](https://github.com/laramies/theHarvester) – Gather emails, subdomains, hosts, and employee names from public sources.

## 🔍 Multi Search

*Tools that perform searches over multiple sources (social media, domains, IPs, phone numbers, etc).*

- [Intelligence X](https://intelx.io/) – Search engine and data archive for OSINT across leaked data, domains, emails, and more.
- [Maltego](https://www.maltego.com) – Provides a library of transforms for OSINT discovery and visualizes information in graph format for link analysis.
- [Recon-ng](https://github.com/lanmaster53/recon-ng) – Full-featured web reconnaissance framework with independent modules and database interaction.
- [sn0int](https://github.com/kpcyrd/sn0int) – Semi-automatic OSINT framework and package manager for gathering intelligence.
- [SpiderFoot](https://github.com/smicallef/spiderfoot) – Automates the collection of OSINT to find everything possible about a target.
- [OSINT Framework](https://osintframework.com/) – Web-based collection of OSINT tools organized by category for investigations.

## 🆔 Username Search

*Tools to check username availability across multiple platforms.*

- [Blackbird](https://github.com/p1ngul1n0/blackbird) – OSINT tool to search for accounts by username across 500+ sites.
- [Forensic OSINT Full Page Screen Capture](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/forensic-osint-full-page/jojaomahhndmeienhjihojidkddkahcn) – Browser extension that captures full web page screens and videos.
- [Maigret](https://github.com/soxoj/maigret) – Collects a dossier on a person by username from thousands of sites.
- [Namechk](https://namechk.com/) – Check username and domain availability across dozens of platforms.
- [Sherlock](https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock) – Search usernames across social networks from the command line.
- [WhatsMyName](https://whatsmyname.app/) – Search usernames across hundreds of websites.

## 📱 Social Media

*Tools for gathering OSINT from popular social media platforms.*

- [Bellingcat TikTok Analysis Tool](https://github.com/bellingcat/tiktok-tool) – Scrape and analyze TikTok data for OSINT.
- [Instaloader](https://instaloader.github.io/) – Download Instagram photos, videos, captions, and metadata.
- [Osintgram](https://github.com/Datalux/Osintgram) – OSINT tool for Instagram that offers analysis of any public profile.
- [snscrape](https://github.com/JustAnotherArchiworst/snscrape) – Social network service scraper for Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, and more.
- [Social Analyzer](https://github.com/qeeqbox/social-analyzer) – Analyze and find profiles across 1000+ social media sites.
- [Twint](https://github.com/twintproject/twint) – Advanced Twitter scraping tool without using Twitter's API.

## 📞 Phone Numbers

*Frameworks and services for investigating phone numbers.*

- [CallerID Test](https://calleridtest.com/) – Free reverse phone lookup service.
- [Carrier Lookup](https://www.carrierlookup.com/) – Determine the carrier for any phone number.
- [NumVerify](https://numverify.com/) – Phone number validation and lookup API.
- [PhoneInfoga](https://github.com/sundowndev/phoneinfoga) – Information gathering framework for phone numbers.
- [Sync.ME](https://sync.me/) – Caller ID and spam blocker with reverse phone lookup.
- [Truecaller](https://www.truecaller.com/) – Caller ID and spam lookup service (commercial).

## 👤 People Search

*Tools and services for finding information about individuals.*

- [411.com](https://www.411.com/) – People search, reverse phone, and address lookup.
- [BeenVerified](https://www.beenverified.com/) – Background check and people search service (commercial).
- [FamilyTreeNow](https://www.familytreenow.com/) – Free genealogy and people search site with public records data.
- [FastPeopleSearch](https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/) – Free people search engine with address, phone, and email lookup.
- [Pipl](https://pipl.com/) – Deep web people search engine that finds information not indexed by standard search engines (commercial).
- [Spokeo](https://www.spokeo.com/) – People search aggregator that pulls data from social networks, public records, and more (commercial).
- [ThatsThem](https://thatsthem.com/) – Free people search with reverse phone, email, address, and IP lookups.
- [WebMii](https://webmii.com/) – People search engine that computes an online visibility score.
- [WhitePages](https://www.whitepages.com/) – Find people, phone numbers, addresses, and background checks.

## 🖼️ Images & Video Analysis

*Tools for reverse image search, metadata extraction, and video analysis.*

- [ExifTool](https://exiftool.org/) – Extract metadata (EXIF, GPS, timestamps) from photos and videos.
- [FaceCheck.ID](https://facecheck.id/) – Reverse image search focused on facial recognition.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) – Multimedia framework for extracting and processing video/audio.
- [Google Images](https://images.google.com/) – Reverse image search.
- [InVID Verification Plugin](https://www.invid-project.eu/tools-and-services/invid-verification-plugin/) – Browser plugin for verifying images and videos (debunking fakes).
- [Jimpl EXIF Viewer](https://jimpl.com/) – Simple online tool for checking image metadata (no install required).
- [PimEyes](https://pimeyes.com/) – Face recognition search engine using uploaded photos (commercial).
- [Surfface](https://surfface.com/) – Face search and people finder that indexes social profiles and other public media.
- [TinEye](https://tineye.com/) – Reverse image search that finds where images appear online and tracks modifications.
- [Yandex Images](https://yandex.com/images/) – Alternative reverse image search with strong face-recognition capabilities.

## 📝 Documentation & Capture

*Tools to preserve findings during an investigation.*

- [Archive.today](https://archive.ph/) – Take a permanent snapshot of any web page for evidentiary purposes.
- [GoWitness](https://github.com/sensepost/gowitness) – CLI tool to take screenshots of web pages for evidence collection.
- [Hunchly](https://www.hunch.ly/) – Web capture tool designed for online investigations that automatically logs and archives pages (commercial).
- [SingleFile](https://github.com/gildas-lormeau/SingleFile) – Browser extension to save a complete page as a single HTML file.
- [Wayback Machine](https://archive.org/web/) – Browse historical snapshots of websites.
- [Webrecorder](https://webrecorder.net/) – Tools for creating high-fidelity interactive recordings of any website.

## 🌍 Geolocation & Mapping

*Resources for geolocation and map-based OSINT.*

- [Bing Maps](https://www.bing.com/maps) – Bird's eye view and street-side imagery.
- [GeoGuessr](https://www.geoguessr.com/) – Geography game useful for training geolocation skills.
- [Google Earth](https://earth.google.com/) – 3D satellite imagery with historical imagery feature for temporal analysis.
- [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/) – Street view and satellite imagery.
- [KartaView](https://kartaview.org/) – Open street-level imagery contributed by a global community.
- [Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com/) – Crowdsourced street-level imagery.
- [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) – Collaborative, free, and editable world map.
- [SunCalc](https://www.suncalc.org/) – Determine sun position for a given time and place (useful for verifying photo timestamps).
- [Wikimapia](https://wikimapia.org/) – Community-annotated map with descriptions of locations worldwide.

## 🌐 Domain/IP/Infrastructure

*Search engines and tools for investigating internet infrastructure.*

- [BuiltWith](https://builtwith.com/) – Technology profiler that shows what websites are built with.
- [Censys](https://censys.com/) – Internet-wide scanning and intelligence platform.
- [crt.sh](https://crt.sh/) – Certificate Transparency log search for discovering subdomains.
- [DNSdumpster](https://dnsdumpster.com/) – Free domain research tool for DNS recon and discovering host records.
- [GreyNoise](https://www.greynoise.io/) – Identifies internet scanners and background noise to separate threats from benign activity.
- [SecurityTrails](https://securitytrails.com/) – Historical DNS data, WHOIS, and domain/IP intelligence.
- [Shodan](https://www.shodan.io/) – Search engine for internet-connected devices.
- [URLScan.io](https://urlscan.io/) – Scan and analyze websites for malicious content and infrastructure.
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) – Collection of free DNS and network tools (reverse IP, port scanner, WHOIS).
- [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/) – Analyze files, URLs, domains, and IPs for malicious content.

## 🌐 Translation

*Tools to help translate and understand foreign-language content.*

- [DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/) – High-quality translation service with strong support for European and Asian languages.
- [Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/) – Widely used machine translation supporting 130+ languages with camera and image translation.

## 📋 Public Records & Court Data

*Tools for accessing public records, court filings, and government data.*

- [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/) – Free legal search engine with millions of court opinions and filings (RECAP project).
- [JUDYRECORDS](https://www.judyrecords.com/) – Free search engine for U.S. court case records.
- [NSOPW](https://www.nsopw.gov/) – National Sex Offender Public Website — search sex offender registries across all U.S. states.
- [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/) – Public Access to Court Electronic Records — U.S. federal court documents.
- [SORA](https://www.nsopw.gov/) – Sex Offender Registry search by state.
- [VINELink](https://www.vinelink.com/) – Nationwide victim notification service — check custody status of offenders.

*Tools for monitoring the dark web and paste sites for relevant intelligence.*

- [Paste Site Search (IntelligenceX)](https://intelx.io/) – Search across paste sites, dark web, and leaked data repositories.

*Tools for tracing cryptocurrency transactions and wallet analysis.*

- [Blockchain Explorer](https://www.blockchain.com/explorer) – Search Bitcoin transactions, blocks, and addresses.
- [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/) – Blockchain analytics platform for tracing cryptocurrency flows (commercial/enterprise).

## 🚗 Vehicle & Property Lookup

*Tools for looking up vehicle registrations, property records, and related information.*

- [FOIA.gov](https://www.foia.gov/) – Submit Freedom of Information Act requests to U.S. federal agencies.
- [NICB VINCheck](https://www.nicb.org/vincheck) – Free service to check if a vehicle has been reported stolen or as a salvage vehicle.
- [SearchQuarry](https://www.searchquarry.com/) – VIN lookup, license plate search, and property records (commercial).
- [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/) – Property lookup with ownership history, tax records, and valuations.

## 🛡️ Threat Intelligence

*Platforms for tracking threat actors, malware, and indicators of compromise.*

- [AlienVault OTX](https://otx.alienvault.com/) – Open threat exchange platform for sharing and researching threat intelligence.
- [Abuse.ch](https://abuse.ch/) – Community-driven threat intelligence on malware and botnets (URLhaus, MalwareBazaar, ThreatFox).
- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) – Knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on real-world observations.
- [Pulsedive](https://pulsedive.com/) – Free threat intelligence platform for searching IOCs and enriching threat data.
- [ThreatCrowd](https://www.threatcrowd.org/) – Search engine for threats — find related domains, IPs, emails, and malware samples.

## 🤖 OSINT Frameworks & Automation

*Comprehensive frameworks and platforms for automating OSINT workflows.*

- [Datasploit](https://github.com/DataSploit/datasploit) – OSINT framework that performs automated recon on domains, emails, usernames, and phones.
- [OSINT Combine](https://www.osintcombine.com/) – Platform with free OSINT tools, training, and resources for investigators.
- [Photon](https://github.com/s0md3v/Photon) – Fast web crawler designed for OSINT that extracts URLs, emails, social media accounts, and files.
- [Raccoon](https://github.com/evyatarmeged/Raccoon) – Offensive security tool for reconnaissance and information gathering.
- [Twint](https://github.com/twintproject/twint) – Advanced Twitter scraping tool that works without API access.
